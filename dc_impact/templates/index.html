How it works

🔌

Energy

Hardware TDP × accelerators × utilization × PUE × duration gives total facility energy in kWh.

💨

Carbon

Energy × regional grid carbon intensity (gCO₂e/kWh) gives CO₂-equivalent emissions.

💧

Water

Energy × Water Usage Effectiveness (WUE in L/kWh) gives onsite cooling water consumption.

🔗

Use POST /api/calculate with JSON to integrate impact reporting into your training scripts or CI/CD pipelines.
